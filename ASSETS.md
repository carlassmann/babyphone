# Pip artwork

`public/pip-sleeping.png` was generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Final prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset: sleeping mascot for Pip, a cute minimal baby monitor PWA. A tiny butter-yellow baby bird asleep, head nestled into its rounded body, one small wing, closed eye drawn in soft ink blue, delicate rosy cheek. Nestled on a small powder-blue crescent cushion. Quiet Scandinavian children's book cut-paper illustration, tactile matte paper with subtle fine grain, simple bold silhouette legible at small sizes. Isolated centered composition with generous empty space, solid very pale blue #edf3fb background. No text, lettering, watermark, mockup, border, extra objects. Square image.

`public/icon.svg` is a hand-built vector companion mark. `public/icon-192.png` and `public/icon-512.png` are raster exports of that SVG using Sharp. The 512px image keeps the bird inside the maskable safe area.

The UI uses locally bundled DM Sans and Fredoka through Fontsource, and Lucide SVG interface icons. No remote fonts or image requests are needed after installation.
